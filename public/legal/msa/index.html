MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date set forth in the applicable Statement of Work ("SOW") by and between:

Pineforge Digital LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("Company"), and [Client Legal Name], ("Client").

Company and Client may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

1. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

1.1
This Agreement governs all services performed by Company for Client.
1.2
Each discrete project shall be defined in a separately executed Statement of Work ("SOW").
1.3
In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, this Agreement shall control unless the SOW expressly states that a specific provision overrides this Agreement.
1.4
No oral statements, proposals, marketing materials, emails, or prior communications modify this Agreement unless expressly incorporated into an executed SOW.

2. SERVICES

2.1
Company provides digital consulting, software development, web development, integration, and related technical services as described in each SOW.
2.2
Company is an independent contractor. Nothing herein creates a partnership, joint venture, fiduciary relationship, employment relationship, or agency relationship between the Parties.
2.3
Company does not guarantee business outcomes, revenue generation, specific commercial results, search engine rankings (SEO), or website traffic volumes unless expressly stated in writing.

3. SCOPE CONTROL

3.1
Services are strictly limited to those expressly and specifically described in an executed SOW.
3.2
Any functionality, feature, integration, deliverable, or service not expressly described in writing in the SOW is excluded, even if reasonably implied, customary in the industry, or discussed during sales or discovery conversations.
3.3
Company is not obligated to perform any out-of-scope work unless the Parties execute a written Change Order.

4. DISCOVERY & TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY

4.1
Client acknowledges that custom software development and third-party integrations may involve unknown technical constraints.
4.2
If, during performance, Company discovers material technical complexity, undocumented system constraints, third-party limitations, inaccurate representations, or integration barriers that materially increase scope or cost, Company may, at its discretion:
  • Issue a written Change Order reflecting revised scope, timeline, and pricing;
  • Convert the affected portion of the project to time-and-materials billing; or
  • Terminate the affected SOW pursuant to Section 13.
4.3
Company shall not be liable for delays or inability to perform caused by third-party platforms, APIs, hosting providers, software vendors, or infrastructure outside Company's control.
4.4
Unless explicitly defined in the SOW, Company develops and tests deliverables for current, officially supported versions of major modern web browsers (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Company is not obligated to ensure compatibility with obsolete, unsupported, or legacy browsers and devices.

5. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Client agrees to:

5.1
Provide accurate, complete, and timely requirements, approvals, and feedback.
5.2
Maintain all rights, licenses, and permissions to content, data, trademarks, and materials provided to Company.
5.3
Provide access to required third-party platforms (e.g., domain registrars, hosting environments) strictly via "Delegated Access," "Collaborator," or "Manager" roles. Company refuses to accept, store, or manage Client master passwords. If Client forces the transmission of master credentials, Client assumes 100% liability for any subsequent security breaches of those accounts.
5.4
Maintain required third-party subscriptions unless expressly included in the SOW.
5.5
Ensure that its business operations comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
5.6
Client acknowledges that Company is not responsible for legal compliance of Client's business, including but not limited to privacy laws, financial regulations, industry regulations, or data protection obligations unless expressly contracted in writing. While Company utilizes standard semantic HTML practices, Client acknowledges that full compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) or Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) requires ongoing third-party audits. Company makes no warranties regarding ADA/WCAG compliance, and Client assumes all legal risk associated with website accessibility.
5.7
Project Dormancy: If Client fails to respond to Company requests, provide required assets, or grant approvals for a period of twenty-one (21) consecutive days, the project will be considered "Dormant." Company reserves the right to reallocate resources. Resuming a Dormant project is subject to Company availability and may incur a project restart fee. If a project remains Dormant for forty-two (42) days, Company may terminate the SOW and invoice for all work completed to date.

Failure to fulfill Client responsibilities may result in project delays or additional fees.

6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1
Fees shall be set forth in the applicable SOW.
6.2
Unless otherwise stated:
  • A non-refundable deposit is required prior to commencement of work.
  • Deposits become earned upon commencement of work.
6.3
Invoices are due upon receipt unless otherwise specified.
6.4
Late payments may accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law.
6.5
Company may suspend work for non-payment. Project timelines shall automatically extend during suspension.
6.6
Client is responsible for all collection costs, including reasonable attorney's fees.
6.7
Payment Processing: Company utilizes third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe) to facilitate transactions. Client agrees to the terms of service of the respective payment processor. Company does not store or process Client's full credit card or bank account information.

7. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

7.1
Any modification to scope, deliverables, timeline, or functionality requires a written Change Order. For the purposes of this Agreement, an email exchange explicitly detailing the additional scope and associated fees, followed by Client's affirmative electronic response (e.g., "Approved" or "Yes, proceed"), shall constitute a valid and binding Change Order.
7.2
Company is not obligated to begin additional work until a Change Order is executed and any required payment is received.
7.3
Repeated scope expansion or excessive revision requests may result in termination at Company's discretion.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1
Upon full payment of all amounts due, Client shall own the final deliverables expressly identified in the applicable SOW.
8.2
Ownership does not transfer until full payment is received.
8.3
Company retains ownership of pre-existing materials, proprietary frameworks, development methodologies, generic code libraries, non-client-specific components, know-how, and technical processes. Client acknowledges that deliverables may contain open-source software, third-party libraries, or frameworks. Company does not transfer ownership of these third-party elements; they remain subject to their respective open-source licenses.
8.4
Company may reuse general knowledge, skills, experience, and non-confidential code elements.
8.5
If payment is reversed, disputed, or charged back, Client's license to use deliverables is automatically revoked.
8.6
Portfolio Rights: Unless expressly revoked in writing by the Client prior to the execution of the SOW, Company retains the non-exclusive right to display graphics, screenshots, and links to the completed project in Company's portfolio, website, social media, and marketing materials as an example of Company's work.

9. WARRANTY AND SUPPORT

9.1
Company warrants that deliverables will materially conform to the written specifications set forth in the applicable SOW for fourteen (14) days following formal acceptance or deployment ("Warranty Period").
9.2
During the Warranty Period, Company's sole obligation is correction of documented defects. A "defect" is strictly defined as code or functionality failing to perform a specific action explicitly outlined in the agreed-upon SOW. Requests to change design elements, add new functionality, or modify features after formal acceptance do not constitute defects and will be billed at Company's standard hourly rate.
9.3
After the Warranty Period, all work is billable unless covered by a separate written maintenance agreement.
9.4
Company does not warrant:
  • Third-party systems, API uptime, or hosting reliability.
  • Browser updates or software updates made after deployment.
  • Modifications made by Client or third parties.
  • Security breaches, malware infections, or unauthorized access resulting from Client negligence, compromised credentials, or third-party vulnerabilities.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1
Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of non-public information disclosed by the other Party.
10.2
Confidential information excludes information that is publicly available or independently developed.
10.3
These obligations survive termination of this Agreement.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1
To the maximum extent permitted by law, Company's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client under the applicable SOW giving rise to the claim.
11.2
In no event shall Company be liable for lost profits, lost revenue, loss of data, business interruption, or any consequential, incidental, indirect, special, or punitive damages.
11.3
These limitations apply regardless of the legal theory asserted.

12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1
Client shall indemnify and hold harmless Company from any claims arising out of Client-provided content or data, Client's business operations, regulatory violations, or misuse of deliverables.
12.2
Company shall indemnify Client solely for third-party claims that deliverables infringe intellectual property rights, provided that the deliverables were created solely by Company and Client has not modified the deliverables.
12.3
Indemnification obligations are subject to the liability cap in Section 11.

13. TERMINATION

13.1
Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for material breach if the breach is not cured within fourteen (14) days after written notice.
13.2
Client Termination for Convenience: Client may terminate at any time upon written notice. Client shall pay for all work performed to date, non-recoverable expenses, and committed third-party costs. There are no refunds for completed phases.
13.3
Company Termination for Convenience: Company may terminate upon written notice. Client shall receive a refund of prepaid fees for unperformed work only.
13.4
Termination Due to Technical Infeasibility: If a project becomes technically infeasible or materially different from Client's representations, Company may terminate the affected SOW. Client shall pay for work performed to date. Company shall have no further liability.
13.5
Upon termination, Client shall cease use of incomplete deliverables. If Company, at its sole discretion, agrees to release incomplete code or designs upon receipt of final payment, such deliverables are provided strictly "AS-IS" without any warranties, guarantees, or ongoing support.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1
The Parties agree to attempt good faith resolution.
14.2
If unresolved, the Parties agree to mediation prior to litigation, except for claims regarding non-payment of fees that fall within the jurisdiction of Wisconsin small claims court, which Company may pursue directly.
14.3
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wisconsin.
14.4
Venue shall be exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wisconsin.

15. NON-DISPARAGEMENT

The Parties agree not to publicly disparage one another during or after a dispute.

16. GENERAL PROVISIONS

16.1
Entire Agreement: This Agreement and executed SOWs constitute the entire agreement.
16.2
Amendments must be in writing and signed.
16.3
Assignment: Client may not assign without Company's written consent.
16.4
Severability: Invalid provisions shall not affect remaining provisions.
16.5
Survival: Sections relating to payment, IP, confidentiality, liability, and indemnification survive termination.
16.6
Electronic Signatures are valid and binding.
