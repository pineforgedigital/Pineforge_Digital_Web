How We Work

A predictable, transparent process designed to reduce risk and ensure we solve the right problem.

1

Inquiry & Discovery

We start with a conversation—email or a call. We discuss your business, your pain points, and what you’re hoping to achieve. We aren't trying to sell you a product; we're trying to determine if we can actually help. If an off-the-shelf tool (like Shopify or Zapier) is better for you, we will tell you.

2

Scope Definition & Proposal

Before writing code, we define the scope. We provide a plain-English proposal that outlines exactly what will be built, the technology stack, the timeline, and the cost. There are no hidden fees or surprise billings.

3

Design & Development

We build in iterative stages. Rather than vanishing for months, we check in regularly. We may share wireframes, prototypes, or staging links so you can see progress. This rigorous feedback loop ensures the software matches your actual workflow.

4

Review & Revisions

Once the core functionality is built, we move to testing. We work with you to verify that everything functions as expected. We squash bugs, refine the interface, and polish the details before going live.

5

Launch & Handoff

We handle the deployment to your production environment (or ours). We set up domain names, SSL certificates, and hosting. We verify that everything is stable and secure, then hand over the keys (and the documentation).

Optional: Maintenance & Support

Software needs care. We offer ongoing maintenance agreements to handle security updates, server monitoring, and small feature additions so your system stays healthy.
