Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Overview

Pineforge Digital LLC respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the limited circumstances under which it is stored.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when you contact us through our website. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

In addition, we use a minimal, first-party cookie (`visitor_id`) to understand general site usage by counting unique visits. This identifier is anonymous, does not track individual behavior, and is not shared with third-party advertisers or analytics platforms.

3. How Information Is Used

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry or communicate with you regarding services, projects, or follow-up questions. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Submitted information is stored only as long as reasonably necessary to manage communication and business records. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we receive, though no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Cookies

Our website uses a single, first-party cookie for basic site analytics. This cookie does not collect personal data and exists only to help us understand overall traffic patterns.

6. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or data brokers. Any infrastructure or email services used are strictly for operational purposes and are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly.
