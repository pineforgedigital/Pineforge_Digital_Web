Who We Are

About Pineforge

Local dedication. Global engineering standards.

Rooted in Wisconsin, Building for Small Business.

Pineforge Digital was founded by Caleb Cannon and Richard Firkin on a simple principle: local businesses in Southeastern Wisconsin deserve high-end, custom websites without the bloat, jargon, and massive overhead of traditional downtown digital agencies.

Based right here in the Kenosha/Racine area, we don't offshore our work and we don't rely on cookie-cutter, drag-and-drop website builders. We hand-code clean, fast, and SEO-optimized websites that are designed to help your business stand out and grow.

Our Core Values

Performance First

We hand-code our websites to ensure blazing-fast load times. A fast website provides a better user experience, ranks higher on Google, and converts more visitors into paying customers.

Tailored Design

No generic templates. We design and build every website from the ground up to perfectly match your brand and operational needs, ensuring you stand out from your local competitors.

Total Ownership

When the project is done, you own 100% of your website and its intellectual property. We believe in earning your business long-term, not holding your site hostage with proprietary platforms.
