Our Mission

Precision Craftsmanship.
Wisconsin Roots.

We are two dedicated web developers building premium, high-performance digital infrastructure for growing local businesses.

A different approach.

Pineforge Digital was built to give local businesses access to agency-level web development without the inflated enterprise price tags.

We noticed too many businesses struggling with slow, generic websites that didn't actually generate leads. They were stuck paying monthly fees for pre-built templates that they didn't fully own or control.

We do things differently. Every platform we build is hand-coded from scratch. No bloated templates, no clunky page builders, and no vendor lock-in. This approach guarantees lightning-fast load times, flawless mobile performance, and a digital storefront built entirely around your unique business goals.

Our Development Philosophy

100% In-House Development

Every line of code is written right here in Wisconsin. We don't offshore or outsource. When you partner with us, you work directly with the developers building your platform.

Obsessive Performance

Speed is not a luxury; it is a requirement. We leverage modern edge networks and custom-built static generation to guarantee instant load times, ensuring you never lose a customer to a slow page.

Unrestricted Ownership

We believe in transparent partnerships, not vendor lock-in. Once a project is completed, you own the entire codebase and all intellectual property. We build it, but it belongs to you.
