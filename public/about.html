About Us

Pineforge Digital LLC is a Wisconsin-based software company focused on designing and developing internally owned digital products. We build applications, platforms, and supporting infrastructure intended for long-term use, maintainability, and real-world reliability.

Who We Are

Pineforge Digital LLC is a privately held software company based in Wisconsin. We are a small, technical team focused on building reliable digital products and custom software systems.

We work directly with business owners and stakeholders to solve operational problems. We do not have sales teams or account managers—when you work with us, you are communicating directly with the engineers designing and building your software.

Our approach is grounded in clarity and maintainability. We don't chase trends or over-engineer solutions. Instead, we build software that is documented, stable, and designed to support your business for the long term.

Our Standards

We believe that good software is:

  • Clear: It solves the problem without unnecessary complexity.
  • Reliable: It works consistently in real-world conditions.
  • Maintainable: It is written to be understood and improved over time.

Partnership

We are looking for long-term partnerships with businesses that value thoughtful engineering. We are not a "churn and burn" agency. We prefer to work with a small number of clients to ensure every project receives the attention and detail it requires.

If you are looking for a dedicated technical partner to help you build, launch, and maintain critical software, we would welcome the conversation.
