About Us

Pineforge Digital LLC is a software company based in Wisconsin, USA. We exist to build useful, durable software products without the hype.

Our Mission

In an industry often obsessed with rapid scaling and temporary trends, we focus on craftsmanship and utility. We build our own products, meaning we are invested in their long-term success and quality.

Why "Pineforge"?

The name represents two concepts: the durability and growth of nature (Pine) and the deliberate, skilled creation of tools (Forge). We believe software should be as reliable as a physical tool.

The Team

We are a small, privately held team of developers and designers. By keeping our team small, we maintain clear communication and a unified vision for our products.
