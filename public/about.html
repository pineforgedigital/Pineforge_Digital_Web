Our Mission

Engineering the Digital Future
of Wisconsin Business.

We build enterprise-grade software scaled for local industry.

The local web design industry is broken.

Local businesses are being drastically overcharged by traditional agencies for slow, bloated, drag-and-drop templates.

Pineforge Digital was founded on a simple realization: small and mid-sized businesses deserve the same caliber of engineering as Silicon Valley startups.

We don't use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace. We are software engineers. When you hire us, you aren't getting a customized $50 template—you are getting a high-performance, custom-coded digital asset built from the ground up to generate revenue and outrank your competitors.

Our Engineering Philosophy

100% In-House

We never offshore our code. Every single line of architecture is written locally in Southeastern Wisconsin. When you call us, you speak directly to the engineers building your product.

Performance Obsessed

Speed is the number one metric for conversion. We utilize static site generation and edge networks to ensure your website loads instantly, no matter where your customers are.

Total Ownership

Many agencies trap you in proprietary software. We don't. Once your project is paid in full, you own 100% of the codebase, the intellectual property, and the database.
