Featured Case Study

Interior Design & Construction Firm

How we built a lightning-fast, premium digital storefront to drive high-ticket leads.

The Challenge

The client is a premium design and construction firm in Southeastern Wisconsin handling high-ticket remodeling projects. They needed a digital presence that reflected the luxury and quality of their physical work. Their main requirement was a portfolio that could display dozens of high-resolution before-and-after images without suffering from slow load times, which often drives potential customers away.

The Solution

We built a custom-coded website from the ground up. Instead of a bloated WordPress template, we utilized a modern architecture (React & Next.js) paired with a headless Content Management System (Sanity CMS). This allowed the client to easily upload massive project photos while the code automatically optimized and compressed them on the fly, keeping the site blazing fast and Google-friendly.
