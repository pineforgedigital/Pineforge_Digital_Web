Common Questions

FAQ

What does a typical project timeline look like?

Most custom software projects take between 4 and 12 weeks, depending on complexity. We will provide a specific timeline during the proposal phase.

Who owns the final code?

You do. Upon final payment, 100% of the intellectual property and source code is transferred to your organization. We do not hold your data.

What happens after launch?

We offer optional maintenance agreements to handle security updates, hosting, and minor changes. You are not required to use us for hosting, but we are available to support the system long-term.

What does the initial conversation involve?

It is a brief phone call or email exchange to see if we are a good fit. We will ask about your business problems and technical needs. If we can't help, we will tell you.

Our Philosophy

How We Work

1. Inquiry & Discovery

We start with a conversation to understand your business and the problem you are trying to solve. We will tell you upfront if custom software is the right solution or if an off-the-shelf tool would be better.

2. Scope & Planning

We define exactly what will be built, how it will work, and what it will cost. You receive a clear technical plan in plain English before any code is written.

3. Development & Review

We build the software in iterative stages. You see progress regularly, not just at the end. This ensures the final product actually matches your operational needs.

4. Launch & Support

We handle the deployment, hosting setup, and handoff. We remain available for long-term maintenance, updates, and support as your business grows.
